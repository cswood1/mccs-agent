Try the assistant on the live Quantico site

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

MCCS Assistant (local)

  1. Keep npm run dev running on port 3000.
  2. Open quantico.usmc-mccs.org in Chrome or Firefox.
  3. Click the bookmark. The “Ask me anything” button appears in the corner.
  4. If Chrome asks to let the site access devices on your local network, click Allow. That's how the page reaches your localhost server.

Nothing is changed on the real site. The widget exists only in your browser tab until you reload.
